# Unit 1A source-grounded fixture

`curriculum.json` is the version 1.0 example: 47 concepts, 59 original exercise seeds (18 choice, 35 text-input, 2 word-tiles, 2 reading, 2 writing). Validate with `npm run validate`.

Source basis: supplied textbook pp. 18 (reading theme), 20 (transport and holiday places), 21 (regular simple past), 22 (irregular forms and present/past), 25 (WH scaffolding and descriptive writing). See references/textbook/MANIFEST.md and experiment/curriculum-research.md. The fixture has no copied textbook passage, chant, source image, supplied-audio substitute or branded reference asset.

Vocabulary includes all nine transport words and the page-20 holiday-place inventory. Irregular verbs have individual stable concepts. Grammar covers regular suffix rules, irregular forms, by + transport, present/past contrast and WH questions. Reading passages and holiday descriptions are new compositions. Pronunciation practice and the extended vocabulary/practice pages remain possible additions; no source audio was supplied.
