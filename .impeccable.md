# Impeccable design context

## Design Context

### Users
German-speaking parents, not technical, deciding at the kitchen table whether to hand a
tool to their child. They arrive with a concrete job: get next week's vocabulary or grammar
practised with the material already in the child's exercise book. Secondary audience: the
child, who never sees this page but is the reason the parent is reading it. Context of use
is short and sceptical — mostly phone, often evening, with other tabs open. The page has one
job: make the parent understand what Lernpfad is and press **Lernpfad öffnen**.

### Brand Personality
Warm, honest, handmade. Closer to a workshop than to software — friendly and inviting
without turning childish. The voice states limits before selling benefits ("Was Lernpfad
nicht ist"); trust is earned by admitting what is unverified. Emotional goal: a parent feels
welcomed and unthreatened, not marketed to.

### Aesthetic Direction
Paper-warm light theme (`--canvas #f7f3ea`), deep pine action colour (`--action #066e66`),
sun and coral accents, `--route` sage for the trail. The hiking-trail metaphor is the
product's own and should carry real structural weight, not appear as decoration.
Direction approved: **noticeably more distinctive** — a self-hosted display face, stronger
typographic contrast, fewer generic cards, bolder composition. Anti-references: SaaS
landing-page template, dark mode with glowing accents, ed-tech gamification (streaks,
confetti, mascots), stock-photo cheerfulness.

### Hard Constraints
- **No external font or CDN request, ever.** A page promising that nothing leaves the device
  must not call `fonts.gstatic.com`. Self-hosted or system stack only.
- **No JavaScript on the landing page.** Static, no app bundle; interaction via native
  HTML/CSS only (`<details>`, `:target`, CSS).
- WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard operation, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected.
- Every claim must map to shipped, verified behaviour (see `experiment/welcome-page.md`).

### Design Principles
1. **Honesty is the aesthetic.** No effect that contradicts a promise on the page.
2. **The trail is structure, not ornament.** Sequence carries information; draw it that way.
3. **One saturated moment.** The privacy block is the page's single strong colour; everything
   else stays on paper.
4. **Warm, never cute.** Children are the subject, not the audience.
5. **Earn the click by stating limits.** Say what it is not, before the parent finds out.
